Data Health Check

Know exactly where your data stands, in two weeks.

A fixed-price review of your data, systems and reporting, with a clear roadmap for what to fix first.

Book your Health Check
What we review

Five things, looked at closely.

  • How data is collected, stored and shared across your organization.
  • How indicators or KPIs are calculated, and whether they're reliable.
  • How reports are produced, and how many staff hours they take.
  • Data quality, security and data protection risks.
  • Where AI or automation could save time.
What you receive

A roadmap you can act on.

  • A visual map of your current data flows.
  • A findings report with the top risks and bottlenecks.
  • An estimate of staff time currently lost to manual work.
  • A prioritized roadmap with quick wins, costs and timelines.
  • A mockup of what your future dashboard could look like.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to change our current tools?

No. We work with what you have and only recommend changes where they pay off.

Is our data safe during the review?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement and work with anonymized samples where possible.

What happens after?

You keep the roadmap. You can implement it yourselves or ask us to build it.
