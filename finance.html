Common challenges

Where lenders lose time and money.

Hidden loan problems cost you your own money. These are the patterns we see most.

  • Loan and member data is spread across a core banking system, mobile money records and spreadsheets.
  • Regulatory and board reports are assembled manually each month.
  • Portfolio risk (arrears, PAR, concentration) is visible only after the month closes.
  • Credit decisions rely on judgment alone, without data-driven scoring.
What we build

Tools for the people who answer for the portfolio.

Portfolio dashboards

Real-time views of disbursements, repayments, portfolio at risk and branch performance.

Early-warning alerts

Flags for loans likely to fall into arrears, so recovery starts before losses grow.

Regulatory and board reporting automation

Reports generated in the required formats on schedule.

SACCO network oversight

One dashboard for unions, banks or funders overseeing many SACCOs, tracking lending, repayment and data quality across all of them.

Member and customer analytics

Retention, product uptake and segment profitability.

Credit-risk scoring

Data-driven scores built from repayment and mobile money history.

AI document processing

Faster onboarding by extracting data from application forms and IDs.
