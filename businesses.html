Common challenges

Compliance first. Then visibility.

Legal compliance and protection against theft and stock leakage are the same data problem.

  • Your invoicing or ERP system isn't connected to EFRIS, so staff re-enter every sale by hand.
  • Input VAT claims fail because supplier invoices don't match EFRIS records.
  • Stock goes missing and you only find out at month end.
  • You can't see which branch, product or salesperson is actually making money.
What we build

From fiscalized sales to live profit views.

EFRIS system-to-system integration

Connect your existing sales, POS or ERP system to EFRIS so every sale is fiscalized automatically.

Sales and profit dashboards

Daily performance by branch, product and staff member.

Stock and leakage alerts
